Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I get started?

You can get started within 24-48 hours of signing up. We'll match you with the perfect virtual assistant and have an onboarding call to ensure a smooth transition.

Can I change my package later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade your package at any time. We're flexible and want to ensure you have the right level of support for your needs.

What if I'm not satisfied?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not completely satisfied with our service, we'll refund your first month's payment, no questions asked.

Are my tasks and data secure?

Yes! We take security seriously. All our virtual assistants sign NDAs, and we use enterprise-grade security measures to protect your data and confidential information.

What happens if I don't use all my hours?

Unused hours roll over to the next month for up to 3 months, giving you flexibility to use your time when you need it most.
